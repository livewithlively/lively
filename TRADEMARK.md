## Please do not, without written permission

- Name your fork, product, company, or service "Lively", or a name likely to be confused with it.
- Use the Marks or logo as the primary branding of your distribution, so that users could believe

- Imply that we endorse, sponsor, certify, or support your product.

**If you redistribute a modified version, please rename it.** That is the entire ask. Keep the
license notices, change the branding.

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, ask — we would much rather answer a question than send a
complaint. Requests for permission and questions about this policy: open an issue, or contact the
maintainers.
